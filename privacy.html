Privacy policy

PSL Email Tool. Effective November 1, 2026.

This policy explains what PSL Email Tool (“the tool”) does with data from your Google account. The tool is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer.

Data the tool accesses

With your permission, the tool uses the Google Sheets API (scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets) to read the shipment tracker spreadsheet you choose in the tool's settings, and to write short status marks back to that spreadsheet, such as marking a shipment as “sent” or recording the person in charge. It does not open any other spreadsheet, and it does not access Gmail, Google Drive files or any other Google service.

How the data is used

Spreadsheet data is used only to build draft emails in your own Microsoft Outlook and to update the status of shipments in your tracker. All processing happens on your computer.

Storage and sharing

The tool has no server. Your Google data is not sent to the developer or to any third party. The sign-in token issued by Google is stored only on your computer, in your Windows user profile folder, so that you do not have to sign in every time. Deleting that folder removes it.

Limited use

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train any model.

Removing access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, the tool can no longer read or write your spreadsheets until you sign in again.
